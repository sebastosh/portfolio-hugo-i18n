*Life Between Borders: The Nomadic Life of Curators and Artists*, edited by Steven Rand and Heather Felty and published by apexart in 2014, collects essays on mobility in contemporary art — on community, artistic presence, and the nomad's existence. ISBN 978-1-933347-65-3.

My contribution is an essay on the heightened attention travel produces: arrivals and departures stay vivid in memory, while the details of an ordinary day at home disappear. The book credits me as Director of Residency Unlimited.

Other contributors include Melissa Chiu, Gitanjali Dang, Jimmie Durham, Mahita El Bacha Urieta, Mekbib Gemeda, Pascal Gielen, Lamia Joreige, Niels Van Tomme, and Yannis Ziogas.
