Co-participation in Flux Factory's installation and performance piece for the Queens Museum of Art's exhibition *Queens International*.

For nearly three months the collective worked inside the gallery in orange jumpsuits, building and maintaining a structure that grew, developed, and then devolved. It was updated daily and documented continuously, until the documentation outweighed the living structure and was all that remained.
