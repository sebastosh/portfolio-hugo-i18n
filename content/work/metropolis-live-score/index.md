Conceived and performed a live sound accompaniment for Fritz Lang's silent film *Metropolis*, in collaboration with the Diagonal cinemas and the DRAC (Direction Régionale des Affaires Culturelles). Covered in the local press.

Diagonal is an independent cinema in downtown Montpellier, open since 1983.
