Flux Factory is a New York arts collective and non-profit organization supporting emerging artists through residencies, exhibitions, and collaborative projects.

- Key stakeholder during a pivotal period in the collective's growth.
- Produced public program graphics and design for digital and print, and documented events and exhibitions.
- Handled sound engineering, audio/visual editing, and technical management for events and exhibitions.
- Produced cultural events and public programming both in-house and throughout the NYC metropolitan area.
- Built and launched the organization's website using WordPress as a CMS.
- Designed the organization's logo, still in use today.
