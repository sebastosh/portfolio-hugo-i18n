Open Stitch was conceived and organized with Jessie Cohan. Fifteen artists spent seven days making wearable garments at Location One under restricted conditions: working only with the materials provided, away from their own studios, and under collaborative constraints that deliberately blurred the line between art and fashion.

Production was streamed live for the duration of the seven days. The finished garments and the video documentation were then installed in the gallery.

Also took part as an artist with Wikiwikicorp, alongside Jean Barberis and Aya Kakeda.

With Ayah Bdeir, Jessie Cohan, Barry Doss, Stefany Anne Golberg, George Hudacko, Selma Karaca, Ryan Kennedy, Miranti Kisdarjono, Katherine Moriwaki, David Quinn, Chris Sanders, Davina Semo, and Wikiwikicorp.
