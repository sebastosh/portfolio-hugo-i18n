A window installation for *Disenchanted Forest*, a summer group show at Flux Factory with Jason D. Brown, Paul Burn, and Aya Kakeda.

The exhibition took an ironic view of nature preservation, treating nature as something best kept in a jar at the natural history museum, and set museum-like contents loose in an open gallery.
