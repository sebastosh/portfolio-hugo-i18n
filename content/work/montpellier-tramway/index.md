Designed and presented a photographic project to decorate the Hôtel de Ville stop in downtown Montpellier, on the city's new tramway line, completed in June 2000.

The project was chosen from 40 candidates while studying for the Diplôme National des Arts Plastiques at the École des Beaux-Arts de Montpellier District. Covered in the local press.
