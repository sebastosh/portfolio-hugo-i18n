Ran a web design workshop during the Biennale des Jeunes Créateurs in Sarajevo, a biennial contemporary art exhibition joining major cities around the Mediterranean, and represented the cultural website Panoplie there.

The tenth edition, held in Sarajevo in the summer of 2001 under the title *Chaos and Communication*, brought together more than 700 artists from 28 countries across design, music, film, literature, visual art, gastronomy, and performing arts. The international BJCEM association was founded during it.

Panoplie was a Montpellier association behind panoplie.org, an online journal of contemporary creation that paired a web-creation research lab with multimedia workshops. It went on to win the multimedia creation prize at Vidéoformes 2003.
