*Miracle on 43rd Street: the tour & the mischief* took participants by bus to the BQE end of 43rd Street in Queens and left them to walk back to Flux Factory with a map they were given. Flux Factory described the street as "the Yellow Brick Road without the colors or the bricks and straighter" — the catch being the map itself.

Recorded on an earlier CV as *Miracle Mischief*, a film.
