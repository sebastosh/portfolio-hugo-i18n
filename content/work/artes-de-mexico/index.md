Artes de México is a Mexico City publishing house. Its magazine, relaunched in 1988 by Alberto Ruy Sánchez, publishes bilingual Spanish–English issues, each devoted to a single theme from Mexico's art, craft, and cultural heritage.

- Production, research, and design assistant for a bimonthly cultural magazine publication.
- Supported documentation and field research photography.
- Published advertisements and promotional material; handled film and print scanning and proofreading in Adobe Photoshop and QuarkXPress.
