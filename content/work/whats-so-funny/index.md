Co-produced videos and accompanied them with live music for the annual arts festival at C.E.S.T.A., the Cultural Exchange Station in Tábor.

C.E.S.T.A. is an international non-profit arts center founded in 1993 by Chris Rankin and Hilary Binder of the duo Sabot, running festivals, residencies, and artist exchanges.
