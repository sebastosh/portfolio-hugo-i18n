A collaborative sound installation in Flux Factory's gallery space and experimental studio. Members of the collective and outside artists developed a number of pieces that interacted with one another while also standing as independent works.

With Jean Barberis, Simon Boudvin, Stefany Anne Golberg, Geraldine Longueville, Morgan Meis, Jason Nunez, Bruno Persat, Marco Scoffier & Miwa, and Sebastien Sanz de Santamaria.

The project returned as *Scape 2* in 2004.
