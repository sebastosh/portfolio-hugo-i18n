A walk-through song made of sound installations, all working from the same beat and drone. Each artist developed sound pieces that interact with one another by following a rhythm of 60 beats per minute over a drone on the note D.

With Pierre Bastien, Guillermo Brown, free103point9, Marco & Miwa, Stefany Anne Golberg with Morgan Meis, and Bruno Persat & Sebastien Sanz de Santamaria.
