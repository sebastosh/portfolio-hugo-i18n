Advisory committee member for SHIFT, a residency at EFA Project Space supporting arts administrators who sustain their own creative practice alongside their institutional work.

Founded in 2010, SHIFT gives artists who work inside arts organizations (curators, educators, administrators) a year of studio space at the Elizabeth Foundation for the Arts, peer support and mentoring, and a closing group exhibition at EFA Project Space. The advisory committee juries the residency and the Project Space's open calls.
