Location One was a non-profit arts space in SoHo, New York, running an international visiting artist studio program alongside exhibitions and public programming.

Residents were nominated by partner foundations and arts organizations sponsoring artists from their home countries, for residencies of five or ten months.

- Joined as program assistant, installing exhibitions and managing art talks and musical performances, then promoted to Technical/Creative Coordinator (2004–2006), coordinating the 10-studio international residency and giving technical and software support to resident artists.
- Maintained an international visiting artist studio program supporting 50+ individual artists from more than 15 countries.
- Produced public programming and events, and installed exhibitions.
- Transitioned the organization's primary website into a WordPress content management system.
